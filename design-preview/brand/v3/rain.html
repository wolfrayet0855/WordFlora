Reduce Motion variant

When Reduce Motion is on there is no falling and no sway: the garden and its already-settled blossoms and plants cross-fade in (0.5 s), then the wordmark fades in (0.3 to 0.8 s). Total 1.0 s, still tappable to skip. Celebration under Reduce Motion: a static settled-blossom frame with the message fading in, no falling particles (use haptic and sound instead if enabled). Spec details and notes

TopicSpec
Canvas390 x 844 pt logical; draw at screen scale. Layers back to front: ground, far blossoms, plant sprites, mid and near blossoms, wordmark (UI layer, above particles so text is never covered).
Motion modelFall: y = lerp(-40, landY, u^1.12) with u = elapsed/fall (a slight ease-in so nothing feels like a constant-speed scroll). Sway: x = x0 + A sin(2pi f t + phase), damped to 0 over the last 14% of the fall (easeOutCubic). Rotation: spin over the fall, damped to the resting angle on landing. Landing: vertical scale eases to 0.55 (petal lies flat), alpha 1 to 0.92, then rests.
Plant sprites5 of the real plant SVGs (daisy, tulip, sunflower, poppy, cherry blossom, marigold, lavender, hibiscus; a seeded pick) fall at 46 px tall, then "plant themselves" with a 0.5 s easeOutBack grow to 92 px standing in the ground. They never overlap the wordmark (it sits at y 250, ground starts at y 694).
GroundSoft grass and soil band rises 0 to 150 pt over 0.1 to 1.0 s (easeOutCubic). Light: #6DB06A grass over #7A5A42 soil. Dark: #3E7A3A grass over #3A2E22 soil.
WordmarkFades in 0.45 to 1.15 s, easeOutCubic, 10 pt rise. Contrast checked on both themes (see brand index).
Palette#F4A6B7, #E8586F, #F98FA2, #E65C4F, #E88A2E, #F2B53E, #FBF6E6, #B79AE0, #8E6BC9, #F7D3DC (sampled from the plant art). In dark theme blossoms are lightened 12% so they do not sink into #1A2E1A. Celebration adds gold sparkles #F0C060 / #FFE08A. Pink is one of ten colours, not dominant.
Calm rulesMax about 2 particles visible per 100 pt column at once; no particle faster than 0.7 screen heights per second; no flashing; fade-in over the first 8% of each fall so particles never pop in; wordmark and tap target never occluded.
CelebrationAbout 2.5x denser (80 blossoms + 22 sparkles), 3.4 s, faster near layer, no ground: particles fall through and fade out so the screen underneath (win result, streak) stays usable. Message card fades in at 0.25 to 0.8 s and out in the last 0.6 s. Reuse for win and streak milestones (streak variant swaps the message only).
Skip and a11yTap anywhere jumps to the final frame and navigates on (Home). Reduce Motion: see above. VoiceOver: announce "WordFlora" only, particles are decorative (hidden). Never block input longer than the skippable 2.4 s; do not replay on every foreground, only on cold start.
PerformanceAbout 37 draw calls per frame standard, about 105 celebration. Pre-render each sprite kind to an offscreen bitmap per colour (10 colours x 4 kinds); avoid per-frame blur filters (the blur here only on the far and near layers: pre-blur the cached bitmaps instead).
