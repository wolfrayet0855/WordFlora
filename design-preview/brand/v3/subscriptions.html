Parental gate (under-13 players)
  • Shown before the paywall, Restore, Manage and any external link when the age setting is Under 13 (or "would rather not say").
  • Adult-level challenge: spelled-out numbers typed on a keypad, randomised each time, so it is not guessable by a young child. A clear way back to the game.
  • Apple still requires a grown-up to confirm the purchase on their own authentication, so the gate is additional, not a replacement.

Between-rounds ad moment (free users, after every 3rd completed round)
  • Calm "Quick break" card: ad slot labelled "Ad", a visible 44 pt close button from the first moment, "Report ad", and a subtle "Go ad-free" text link (not a button, not red, no countdown).
  • Never during a round, never after a loss streak, never for subscribers. Under-13: non-personalised, COPPA-compliant ad network only.
  • Continue button is always available: the player can leave at any time.
