R3.2 Next to Duolingo green (#58CC02), honestly

At 60 and 40 px beside a #58CC02 tile

Reading

Hue is similar (about 88 versus 94 degrees), but ours is far less saturated and darker, so it reads olive against Duolingo's neon lime, and the cream flower with a gold smiley is a different silhouette from their owl. Residual risk: in a folder of small icons the green ground alone will still evoke "language-learning green"; the flower is what differentiates, so keep the petals large.

R2.3 iOS 18 appearances and iOS 26 layers

Default

Dark

Tinted (petals lightest, centre darker, face lightest)

Layers for Icon Composer (flat, transparent, no shadows or highlights)

Dark set: layers/dark-1..4-*.svg. All content sits inside a radius of 415 px (81% of the half-canvas), well inside the iOS mask.

  • Default: background + three foreground layers as shown; Icon Composer adds its own specular and depth, so the layers carry none.
  • Dark: use the dark background fill (#183219) and warm cream petals; or leave one set and let the composer darken, but a hand-set dark keeps cream petals from glaring.
  • Clear (iOS 26): the system renders translucent glass: background layer is dropped or made transparent, the petals/centre/face become frosted shapes. This works because layers are separate and the petals outline is already distinct. Check that the face stays readable on glass (it is the darkest layer, so it should be fine).
  • Tinted: the system maps layer luminance to the tint. Set petals = lightest, centre darker, face lightest, as in the tinted preview. Keep the centre clearly darker than the petals so it stays a focal point.

R2.5 Launch screen and wordmark

Following the HIG, the launch screen has no baked text or branding. Recommended: plain colour only (LaunchScreenColor, #FAF7F0 / #1A2E1A, no UIImageName). Optional: the flower alone, centred at 120 pt. The two-tone wordmark moves to the Home title as a vector.

The dotted specks around the flower centre in the old dark art came from petal seams showing the background; the new flower has a filled hub under the petals and no outlined centre, and a 3x crop shows it clean.

Home title wordmark (vector, outlined)

Light: "Word" #2C3E2C (10.7:1), "Flora" #2A7A38 (4.98:1, darkened from #2F8A3E which was 4.07:1). Dark: "Word" #D4EDD4 (11.6:1), "Flora" #F0C060 (8.6:1). Files: v2/wordmark/wordmark-light.svg, wordmark-dark.svg.

Round 1 (superseded): 1. Chosen direction: B, Evergreen

Cream petals on garden green with a gold centre. Neutral, high contrast, no pink. Two options for the user to pick: with the smiling face, or faceless (calmer, more "botanical", loses some of the mascot's personality).

Round 1: 2. iOS 18 variants of B

Any/Light, Dark and Tinted for each option. Dark uses a deep green ground with warm cream petals. Tinted is pure greyscale on black; the preview multiplies it with a sample green.

Round 1: 3. Launch screen (baked wordmark, dropped)

UILaunchScreen supports only a background colour plus a centred static image, so the mark and the "WordFlora" wordmark are baked into one transparent PNG (260 x 250 pt, outlined text). Background colour from LaunchScreenColor (#FAF7F0 light, #1A2E1A dark). Light uses a green disc so the cream flower reads on cream; dark uses a soft lifted-green disc.
