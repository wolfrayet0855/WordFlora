1. Badge system

36 emblems in four families (Collection, Play, Tending, Explorer; Seasonal comes later), each with bronze, silver and gold, 108 emblems in total. Tier is told by metal colour, a notched medal outline and one to three pips, not by colour alone. Family colour sits in the glyph: green Collection, orange Play, blue Tending, purple Explorer. All positive-only; badges are never revoked. SVGs in v4/svg/badges/.
