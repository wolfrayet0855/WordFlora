Design risks to review

  • Tool icons at 40 px: gold and silver tiers are close in lightness on a cream background; pips carry the tier but should be tested at small sizes on device.
  • 108 badge emblems share one medal shape; glyph legibility at shelf size (about 70 px) is fine but the mono-style tool glyphs are the weakest. Consider a second pass if the user wants more personality.
  • Visitor art is deliberately simple; the hummingbird and dragonfly are the roughest. Real-species facts need a final fact-check before shipping.
  • Sticker album greyed silhouettes could feel like missing out; tone is mitigated by neutral copy, no countdowns and no purchase path.
  • Rewards and caps (3 rewarded plays a day) are placeholders for the economy owner.
  • The firefly and luna moth appear only in dark mode, which means light-mode players cannot meet them unless there is a night-time trigger (suggest: time of day or an in-game "evening" toggle).
  • Animations here are web approximations; the SwiftUI port must re-time them and add the haptics and sounds listed.
