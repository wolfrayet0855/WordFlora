5. Net swoosh and rewards

Net swoosh

Arc sweep 0.3 s, golden trail #F2B53E at 70% alpha drawn with a stroke-dash reveal then fade 0.25 s. Tier 2 and 3 nets widen the trail and the catch radius (48 / 64 / 80 pt). A catch plays a sparkle burst (6 stars) and the sticker pop (scale 0.4 to 1.1 to 1, 0.35 s). Haptic: soft impact on swipe, success notification on catch.

Reward table

Per catch: common +5 water; uncommon +1 shed point; rare +1 shed point and +15 water. Round bonus: at least +10 water even with no catches. Daily: first 3 rounds are rewarded; further rounds are for fun. A repeat species gives a smaller reward and a sticker count increment.

Gentle by design

No lives, no losses, no penalties for misses, no timers pressure beyond the calm bar, no pay-to-win, no ads, no chasing in Reduce Motion, no insects shown in distress. Visitors are released after a catch (sticker only).
