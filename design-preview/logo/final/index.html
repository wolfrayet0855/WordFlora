WordFlora wordmark: final

"B uncut", the review team's verdict. Each O is Nunito Black's own "o" with five of A's ellipse petals around it. Only the 36-unit gap around each o's own ring is cut. No neighbouring letter cuts a petal, because each flower O gets extra side bearing until its petals stay clear of the next letter. Everything is plain filled paths, with no mask, stroke or filter.

Why the reach is 1.75×, not 1.40×

At 1.40× only about 70 units of each petal show outside the gap. That is about 1.5 px at 120 px wide, so the Flora petals cannot be counted and the acceptance check fails. At 1.75× all five can be counted.
