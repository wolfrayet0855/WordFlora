# T-F logo review (Sonnet)

**Child, Home screen.** A: yes. The two O's are bold orange and pink flowers with a visible centre. A 7-year-old sees "flowers" at once and still reads "WordFlora". B: the petals are small and thin, and the flower is hard to read as one. Final: the O's are mostly dark letters with faint side petals, so the flower is hard to find. A child would read the word but may not notice the flowers.

**Parent, calm and well made.** Final and B look quieter and more grown-up. B's muted palette is the calmest. A is cheerful and reads closer to clip-art, though the proportions are tidy.

**At 120 px.** A stays crisp. The coloured discs are the clearest thing in the image, and the O is still legible. Final at 120 px is mushy: the petals nearly vanish and the O looks like a smudged dark ring, so the flower idea is lost where the mark is used most.

## Ship this round
A-leaning hybrid. Use A's solid flower O's (disc and petals large enough to survive at 120 px), set in final's calmer two-tone letter colours. The owner's idea only works if the flowers are visible at nav-bar size. A delivers that and final does not. A's saturation is a little loud for the "calm" brief, so soften it rather than abandon it.

## Icon
No, don't replace it now. The flower-with-O icon is attractive at 60 px but weaker at 29 px, where the O turns into a green blob inside a busy flower, and it is too close to the wordmark to add anything. The smiling daisy is still clear, friendly and readable at both sizes. Revisit once the wordmark is settled.

## Fixes for the chosen mark
1. Make the petals at least 40% of the O's outer diameter, with a minimum of 3 px per petal at 120 px wide.
2. Keep all 5 petals visible. Never let the side petals tuck behind the neighbouring letters.
3. Set the centre dot to warm yellow `#F2B84B`, and the petals to `#E9923A` (orange O) and `#E27D8A` (pink O) at slightly lower saturation than A. Letters: `#2F4A2B` for Word and `#3E7A3A` for Flora.
4. Add 2 to 3 px of extra letter-spacing on each side of every flower O so the petals don't crowd W, r, F or r.
5. Check the contrast of the pink flower against the cream background (`#FAF6EC`). The petal outline should be at least 3:1, so darken it by about 10% if it is lower.
