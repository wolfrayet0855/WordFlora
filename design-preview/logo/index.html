Recommendation

Winner: B's ring-O and icon, refined with A's bloom shape, colour logic and kerning. B is the only direction where the word still reads "WordFlora" with the petals removed, and its icon is the only one that links the garden to the word game. A's wordmark is more polished, so the refinement borrows that polish.

  • Petals: five rounded petals on both O's (drop the six-petal daisy, which becomes a starburst at 120 px). Petal tips no more than 1.18× the ring radius. The Word O has a petal pointing up, the Flora O is rotated 36°.
  • Gap: 36 u (up from 30), cut against all glyphs dilated by 36 u, so the petals tuck behind the W and the r.
  • W–o fix: Nunito's native W–o advance minus 40 u. The optical W–o gap should be within ±10% of the r–d gap. The d|F breath is 20 u at most.
  • Petal colours: light #E0962A (4.67:1 against the ink ring) and #EE8C9E (2.25:1 against the green ring, up from 1.68). Dark #E0962A (5.91:1 against the background) and #E25D78 (2.04:1 against the gold ring). Letters are unchanged and all at least 4.5:1.
  • Sizes: twin from 130 pt wide upwards (Home 172 pt, Onboarding 262 pt). Solo (only the Flora O blooms) below 130 pt.
  • Build: bake the gap into plain filled paths (no <mask>, stroke or filter). Ship an SVG image set "Wordmark" (Any and Dark appearances, Preserve Vector Data) plus "WordmarkSolo". WordmarkView becomes an Image with a @ScaledMetric width and keeps .accessibilityLabel("WordFlora").
  • Icon: a candidate only, placed under design-preview/logo/icon/. B's composition with the gradient changed to #3E7A2A → #2E6B22, the ring enlarged 6%, full-bleed with no baked corners. No AppIcon swap this round. I would not be unanimous yet: it loses the current icon's smiling face and has not been checked on a real home screen.

Refined

Shipped: "B uncut". This is the review team's verdict (review-opus.md and review-sonnet.md). The ring is Nunito Black's own "o" with five of A's ellipse petals. Only the 36-unit gap around each o's own ring is cut, and no neighbouring letter cuts a petal. Each flower O gets extra side bearing so its petals stay clear of the next letter. Twin is used from 130 pt wide up, solo below that.
