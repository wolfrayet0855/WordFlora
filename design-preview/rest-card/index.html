Resting card + break card

Three directions for GameView.restCard (after every 3rd regular round or the Daily Bloom) and breakCard (under-13, after 20 minutes). The ad line appears only for a free player when AdGate.shouldShowInterstitial would say yes for "One more"; Plus and under-13 players never see it, and never see the Plus row. Use the controls; all three phones update together. PNGs live in shots/ (sh render.sh).

Honest about ads

  • "A short ad may play first." sits right under "One more", the choice that can lead to an ad. It is never hidden in a footnote.
  • Shown only when AdGate.shouldShowInterstitial(adContext(choice: .oneMore)) is true right now. "may" covers the case where no ad loads.
  • "That's enough for now" never shows an ad, so it gets no line.
  • Plus row: only when app.showBanner (free, 13+, past first session). No price, no timer. Opens the existing parental-gated Plus screen.

Motion

  • Default: card rises and fades in (0.45 s), plant breathes (4.8 s), stars twinkle, two "z"s float. All slow, nothing flashes.
