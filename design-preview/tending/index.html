Tending, pots and life stats

Design review on localhost. Nothing here is built in SwiftUI yet. Art is placeholder-grade vector in the Evergreen palette.

1 Pots2 Tending3 Life stats

1. Pot collection

Ten cosmetic pots, all on the same 120x100 shape so they swap cleanly. Each card shows the unlocked pot with a sample plant, and the locked silhouette (right) the player sees in the Shed. Unlock rules are fixed and predictable, no random drops.

Shed: Pots section (selected pot has a check and a ring; locked ones are dashed with a lock and a plain unlock hint)

Repot sheet: "Pick a pot" row above the soil choices

2. Tending effect storyboards

Each effect is under 2 seconds in the app; the loops here add a rest at the end so you can see the beats. Left is full motion, right is the Reduce Motion version (fades only, nothing travels or scales). Everything is an overlay on the plant, no layout shift.

3. Life stats (replaces the daily capacity ring)

Three options. Toggle the state to see both: thriving, and "could use a little care". Care state never goes red and never reads as empty; meters keep a soft floor and the words carry the meaning, not only the colour. Growth is never lost.

Design questions

  • Should "Happiness" decay at all, or only react to recent care (so a long break never looks sad)?
  • Option a (clear, familiar), b (most emotional, ties to bond hearts), c (shows progression). Pick one, or a + c?
  • Pot unlock order and the two sparkly pots' triggers.
