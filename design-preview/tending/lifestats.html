Life stats panel, rain and petals

The plant is Daisy's own art from the app, kept in the centre. Under her sits a small row: a love flower, then five pink petal pips, one for each day of love she has in store. After a day with no love, a soft rain starts, one of her own petals lets go and drifts down to the ground, and one pip turns to a dashed outline. Any love brings the pips back, one at a time. No numbers, no red, and every state has an icon and words.

The rule (every age)

  • 24 hours with no love: a soft drizzle starts, one of Daisy's own petals lets go and drifts down once (3.4 s) through the rain, blushing love pink, and rests on the ground by her stem. At the same moment one pip in the row fades to a dashed outline. One pip a day; the rain gets steadier (drizzle, light rain, then rain with soft far thunder from day four). The fall plays once a day, never on a loop. The same rule for every age.
  • When no pink pips stay: Daisy tucks herself into her pot as a sleeping seed. The row under her is hidden; the panel shows five dashed pips. Stage, plants, words and streak history are all kept. The rain stops while she sleeps.
  • Waking: the "Wake Daisy" card opens a short wake-up game (wake options). After that, any love (a tap, a word, a tending action) brings the pips back, one at a time, 0.3 s apart.
  • Calm mode and Quiet Garden: no rain, no falling petals and no seed. All five pips stay pink (frame 11).
  • Hearts and petals: hearts are today's love; the pips show how many days of love she has in store. The panel's Love key shows the same five pips with one line, "Love keeps her petals."
  • The love row: a glass pill under Daisy, clear of the cloud, the rain band (drops land above it), the Life panel and the action buttons. Filled pip: love pink with a deeper pink edge. Pip gone for now: a dashed outline with a faint fill, so it shows in dark mode too. Never red, no numbers.
  • VoiceOver: the plant and mood come first, then (in the seed state) the Wake card, then the panel, one container "Life". Meters read "Water, full". Growth reads "Growing, bloom stage". Hearts read "Love, 2 of 5 hearts". Daisy reads "Daisy, 3 of 5 love petals" (the row under her is part of that one element). The panel key reads "3 of 5 love petals. Love keeps her petals." Minimise: "Hide Life panel" (aria-expanded true); tab: "Show Life panel" (aria-expanded false). The app remembers the choice. Weather is hidden from VoiceOver; when rain starts there is one announcement, "A rainy day for Daisy."
  • Reduce Motion: no canvas, no flash, no thunder haptic. A still cloud and a "Gentle rain" badge under the love row. Today's petal does not drift: it fades in where it rests on the ground, and its pip fades. Regrown pips simply appear. The panel fades instead of shrinking.
  • Dynamic Type: at accessibility sizes the panel becomes a Life sheet that scrolls, and Water, Feed, Sun and Repot stay pinned below it as a 2 by 2 bar (64 pt tall each). Daisy moves up, stays centred and stays tappable.
  • Taps: the love row, the falling petal, cloud, rain, badges and floating hearts never catch taps (allowsHitTesting(false)), so a tap anywhere on Daisy reaches her.

Thunder (from parts/weather.html)

  • Light: one slow flash (1.4 s, soft rise), never more than once per 8 seconds (8 to 15 s apart), 16 percent white overlay (10 percent in dark), small pale bolt inside the cloud. No strobes, no red. None in Calm or Quiet Garden.
  • Sound: soft far rumble 2 to 3 s, low-pass about 400 Hz, 150 ms attack, no crack, quiet (about -24 LUFS). It plays when Sound is on and Calm is off. Try it with "Thunder sound".
  • Haptic: one soft light tap 0.3 to 0.5 s after the flash, none in Calm mode.
  • Cost: 30 fps cap, one path per rain layer, colours cached on theme change, canvases sized to the rain band and paused when off screen or when the app is in the background.
