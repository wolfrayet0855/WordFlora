Life stats side panel and the no-love rule

Collapsible panel on the left edge of the plant screen. Open it to see three care meters, the growth path and love hearts; minimise it to a slim heart tab so the plant has the whole screen. No numbers, no red, every state has an icon and a words label. The plant stays centred in every state.

  • Rain starts after one day with no love; a soft cloud and drops fall behind the plant. Reduce Motion: still cloud badge reading "Gentle rain", a petal resting on the ground, no movement.
  • Petals: one lost per extra day. When the last falls the plant becomes a seed in its pot. Progress, stage and plants are kept (hearts bar shows "resting", never empty-and-sad).
  • Waking: a word puzzle or butterfly catching; then any love brings petals back one at a time.
  • VoiceOver: panel is one container "Plant life, expanded". Each meter reads "Water, nourished". Path reads "Stage, bud, 3 of 4". Hearts read "Love, 3 of 5 filled" (counts only for VoiceOver, not shown). Minimise button: "Hide plant life panel"; tab: "Show plant life panel".
  • Dynamic Type: at large sizes the panel grows taller, and at accessibility sizes it becomes a bottom sheet instead of a side strip.
