Life stats panel, rain and petals

The plant is Daisy's own art from the app, kept in the centre. The small crown of five pink petals above her is her health, with a heart in the middle: love keeps her petals. After a day with no love, one petal drifts down and a soft rain starts. Any love brings the petals back, one at a time. No numbers, no red, and every state has an icon and words.

The rule (every age)

  • 24 hours with no love: a soft drizzle starts and the first crown petal drifts down. One more petal each day; the rain gets steadier (drizzle, light rain, then rain with soft far thunder from day four). The same rule for every age.
  • When the crown has no petals: Daisy tucks herself into her pot as a sleeping seed. Stage, plants, words and streak history are all kept. The rain stops while she sleeps.
  • Waking: the "Wake Daisy" card opens a short wake-up game (wake options). After that, any love (a tap, a word, a tending action) brings a petal back, one at a time.
  • Calm mode and Quiet Garden: no rain, no petal loss and no seed. The crown stays full (frame 11).
  • Hearts and petals: hearts are today's love; petals show that love keeps her well. The panel says it in one line, "Love keeps her petals", and the crown has a heart in the middle.
  • VoiceOver: the plant and mood come first, then (in the seed state) the Wake card, then the panel, one container "Life". Meters read "Water, full". Growth reads "Growing, bloom stage". Hearts read "Love, 2 of 5 hearts". The crown reads "Petals, 3 of 5". Minimise: "Hide Life panel" (aria-expanded true); tab: "Show Life panel" (aria-expanded false). The app remembers the choice. Weather is hidden from VoiceOver; when rain starts there is one announcement, "A rainy day for Daisy."
  • Reduce Motion: no canvas, no flash, no thunder haptic. A still cloud, a "Gentle rain" badge under Daisy and one petal resting on the ground. The panel fades instead of shrinking.
  • Dynamic Type: at accessibility sizes the panel becomes a Life sheet that scrolls, and Water, Feed, Sun and Repot stay pinned below it as a 2 by 2 bar (64 pt tall each). Daisy moves up, stays centred and stays tappable.
  • Taps: the crown, cloud, rain, badges and floating hearts never catch taps (allowsHitTesting(false)), so a tap anywhere on Daisy reaches her.

Thunder (from parts/weather.html)

  • Light: one slow flash (1.4 s, soft rise), never more than once per 8 seconds (8 to 15 s apart), 16 percent white overlay (10 percent in dark), small pale bolt inside the cloud. No strobes, no red. None in Calm or Quiet Garden.
  • Sound: soft far rumble 2 to 3 s, low-pass about 400 Hz, 150 ms attack, no crack, quiet (about -24 LUFS). It plays when Sound is on and Calm is off. Try it with "Thunder sound".
  • Haptic: one soft light tap 0.3 to 0.5 s after the flash, none in Calm mode.
  • Cost: 30 fps cap, one path per rain layer, colours cached on theme change, canvases sized to the rain band and paused when off screen or when the app is in the background.
