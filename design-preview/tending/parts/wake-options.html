Wake Daisy: seed, choice, challenge, success, try again

Decisions in notes/decision.md. The seed card has a quiet "Wake Daisy" button. It opens a calm sheet with two big tiles in a stack (no carousel). The suggested tile is chosen by a simple rule, shown first, and focused first. A plain X closes the sheet. A try that does not work out is "Still dreaming", never a failure.

  • 0 Seed card: "Daisy is resting in her seed. She is safe and cosy." One quiet tinted "Wake Daisy" button, no counters, no notifications.
  • 1 Choice sheet: tiles in a stack, suggested first, with the badge "Good for today". Adaptive rule: suggest the game the player used to wake a seed most recently; if there is none, or the most recent try ended in "Still dreaming", suggest butterflies. Use the "Most recent wake try" menu above to preview it. Chips say "Quick" / "A little longer" (no minutes) and "Gentle" / "Tricky" as words. No text "not now" button: a plain 44 pt X in the corner (VoiceOver "Close") and swipe down both close it, with no change and no second ask this session.
  • 2 Challenge intro: one short rule line, "Take your time.", one big Start button. Back returns to the sheet.
  • 3 Success: sprout, glow, then her pink love pips one at a time, 0.3 s apart, 2.4 s at most. The same row as under Daisy on the Life page: a small love flower, then five pips, pink for love and a dashed outline with a faint fill for the rest. "Tap to see them all." skips to all petals. Reduce Motion: all petals at once. Soft haptic on the first petal and on the hearts, and none when haptics are off. One VoiceOver announcement: "Daisy is awake."
  • 4 Still dreaming: "Daisy is still dreaming. You can try again any time." Primary "Try again", secondary "Try the butterflies". After 2 in a row, the next try is easier and butterflies becomes the main button.
  • VoiceOver order: sheet title, suggested tile, other tile, Close. Tile label: "Catch butterflies, quick, gentle, good for today".
  • Dynamic Type: tiles grow, art shrinks to 56 pt, chips wrap. At 5+ challenges or accessibility sizes the sheet uses the .large detent and scrolls.
