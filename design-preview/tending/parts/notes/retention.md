# Retention view: wake the seed (Researcher-Retention)

Sources read: wake-options.html, game-effects-round3.md s5, reinforcement-principles.md, STATUS.md (24 h rule). Web not fetched this pass; every URL below is UNVERIFIED (from memory).

**Q1 Tiles vs swipeable cards: tiles.** Two options need no navigation; both are visible, so choice is autonomy-supportive without hidden content (SDT: autonomy, competence; https://selfdeterminationtheory.org). Swipe hides option 2 and adds a gesture burden for kids and VoiceOver. Keep the pre-focused suggested tile. Confidence: high.

**Q2 Default challenge: butterflies (gentle), adaptive.** Flow needs challenge slightly above skill, but a returning (lapsed) player has rusty skill and is emotionally fragile, so aim for a ~90-95% first-attempt success (UNVERIFIED target; Duolingo-style "easy re-entry" lessons, Csikszentmihalyi 1990). The wake is a reunion, not a test. Rule: default = butterflies for first-ever wake and after any "Still dreaming"; suggest the word puzzle only after the player has won 2+ wakes by word puzzle, or if they chose it last time (remember last choice, stay player-led). Never auto-escalate difficulty inside a single wake.

**Q3 Petals: one at a time, but short.** Peak-end rule (Kahneman; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak%E2%80%93end_rule) says the ending is what is remembered; staggered return with a haptic each gives savouring and builds to the hearts finale. Cap total at ~2.5 s (about 0.25-0.3 s per petal), tap anywhere to skip to all-at-once. Reduce Motion: all at once (already specified). Confidence: high.

**Q4 "Maybe later" and "Still dreaming".**
- Maybe later leaves the seed as it is, no timer, no counter, no badge. Re-surface only as a calm in-garden line ("Daisy is resting"), at most one optional notification per lapse, off by default for under-13s. No streak language (Finch/Forest use gentle, non-shaming lapse copy; UNVERIFIED specifics, https://finchcare.com, https://www.forestapp.cc).
- Retries: unlimited, free, no cooldown. After 2 misses in a row, make the next attempt easier (adaptive assist) and make the gentler challenge the primary button. Confidence: medium-high.

**24 h rule: too punishing; use a safer variant.** Evidence in-repo: reinforcement-principles item 4 promises "no punishment... plants never wilt", and STATUS.md says the petal loss replaces that rule. Loss framing after only one missed day is the trap that Tamagotchi-style games used and that Duolingo's streak guilt is criticised for (UNVERIFIED; loss aversion, Kahneman and Tversky 1979). Kids miss days for ordinary reasons, and the first lapse is a high churn point.
Proposed: **48 h grace, then one petal per day only after the second missed day**; first petal-loss day shows rain with a warm "Daisy misses you" line, never a count of petals lost. Add a "welcome back" tap-hello that restores love instantly and keeps petals, so any return is a win. Keep the exemptions; consider a parent-set pause. Confidence: high on direction; exact 48 h figure is a judgement, test in beta.
