Rose: curled petals

The petals that stay on the head now get sleepy as the pips fall. Each one rolls its tip back into a tube, so the petal gets shorter, and it droops toward the ground about its base. At 4 pips three outer petals stay, curled and drooping a little. At 3 pips a mirrored pair stays beside the closed bud, rolled further and drooping more. The curled rose also peels in a new order so this lasts two pips. At 5 pips nothing changes: it is the same rose as the art. Everything stays in the rose's own reds and nothing is see-through.
