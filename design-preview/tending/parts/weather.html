Gentle rain
Thunder sound spec. Soft distant rumble, 2-3 s, low-pass ~400 Hz, slow 150 ms attack, long tail, no crack. -24 LUFS integrated, peak below -12 dBFS. Plays when Sound is on and Quiet Garden/Calm mode is off; from day 4; mixed under any music.
Haptic. One soft transient (intensity ~0.3, sharpness ~0.2), 0.3-0.5 s after the flash (sound-travel lag), same delay as the rumble start. Skipped in Calm mode.
SwiftUI. Drops: TimelineView(.animation(minimumInterval: 1/30)) + Canvas (struct Drop with layer, x, y, ground y; draw lines and ellipse rings; cap 60). Flash: Rectangle().fill(.white).opacity(flash ? 0.16 : 0) with .animation(.easeInOut(duration: 1.4), value: flash) (0.10 in dark, peak at 25%), plus a small bolt shape in the cloud. Scheduler: Task.sleep 8-15 s. Haptic: .sensoryFeedback(.impact(weight: .light, intensity: 0.3), trigger: thunderCount) fired after a 0.3-0.5 s sleep. Gate everything on @Environment(\.accessibilityReduceMotion): if true show a still cloud and a "Gentle rain" badge, no canvas, no flash.
Safety. Max 1 flash per 8 s (WCAG 2.3.1 allows 3/s); one slow-ramp flash, no double strobes; peak overlay 16% white (10% dark), bolt 28% opacity and small; no red flashes.
