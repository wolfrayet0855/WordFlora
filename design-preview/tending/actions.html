Tend actions: meters, buttons and effects

Fix for "Daisy has had enough sun" when Sunshine is low. Rule: a meter shows how much its own action can do today. Low means the button works. Full means it rests, and the tap says why in kind words. Design review only, not built yet.

1. Meter and button states

Rule from the project plan: each action has its own daily allowance (Water 2 a day, Feed once, Sun once) and its meter shows only that. Not full means the button works. Ready: meter low, button lit with a ring. Water after one drink reads Good and still works. Done for today: meter full, button checked. Full: the button rests (moon badge, striped meter); tapping it says why and what comes next.

2. Tap response (live)

Tap Water, Sun or Soil. The button presses in, the meter fills in a way that matches the action (water ripples, sun glows, soil rises in steps), the scene plays once, and a toast says what happened. A full action shows its reason as the toast and shakes nothing.

3. Tend effects (each under 2 seconds, plays once)

Press Play on each. Frames show the exact moments. Reduce Motion swaps all travel for a 0.4 second fade to the end look.

5. Rules and copy

  • Meter = that action's own allowance today: Water is waterings divided by 2 (0, 50, 100), Sun and Feed are 0 or 100. Not full means the button works. Time since the last action must not drive the bar. No action blocks another.
  • A resting button stays tappable. The tap shows the reason as a toast and a VoiceOver announcement, never a silent refusal.
  • Button stays Feed. Owner decision 3 in the plan: default is to show soil only through Feed's done state. This page also shows an optional Soil meter row; delete it if the default stays.
  • Large text: the same states appear in the pinned 2 by 2 bar. Check and moon badges keep their shape, so colour is never the only signal.
