Garden Visitors

HubShedButterfly game

Twelve real visitors (Butterfly.swift:15): five often seen, four sometimes seen, three rare. Fireflies and luna moths come in the evening (17:30 to 21:30). You meet them in the butterfly game; each hello adds a sticker to the album. Example player: 7 of 12 met.

1. App today App today

There is no Visitors screen of its own: the Home "Visitors" tile opens the butterfly game intro, and the album is a sheet from there (AlbumView, ButterflyView.swift:182). The album shows name and fact for visitors you met; the rest are dark grey shapes with "Not met yet". Rarity and how many times you met each one are stored but never shown.

2. Proposed Proposed

A real Visitors page: how many friends you have met, a big "Say hello" card that starts the game, and the album right there. Album cards show how often a visitor comes (Often seen, Sometimes seen, Rare visitor) and how many times you met it. Visitors not met yet are soft outlines, not dark shapes. A tap opens a card with the fact. An optional pet name lets a child name a visitor, like plants have nicknames.
