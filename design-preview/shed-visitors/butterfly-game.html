Butterfly game

HubShedVisitors

Home → Visitors opens this game (RootView.swift:32). It opens after 3 garden days or the first word level up. One round is 35 seconds. Visitors drift in from the sides; swipe the net (or tap) to say hello. A swing that catches nothing costs nothing, there are no lives and every round ends with a thank-you. The same game wakes a resting seed: catch 3 (2 after two "still dreaming" tries) and the petals come back.

Press and drag across a visitor to swing the net, or just tap one. Keyboard: Tab to a visitor, then Enter (the same as the VoiceOver action in the app).

App today is what ButterflyView.swift and WakeSheet.swift do: a sticker pops for 0.9 s and the name is spoken to VoiceOver but not shown on screen. Proposed adds the name under the sticker, a "New friend!" card the first time you meet a visitor, a friends counter, and a summary that wraps. Calm mode game (proposed) hides the time bar and slows the drift.
