Repot sheet v2

One calm header with Daisy in the picked pot, a 4 x 4 grid of fixed-height tiles that all fit above the pinned button, goals on the pots to earn, and a smaller button that names the pot and the soil. Sheet opens at .large. iPhone 17 frames, 393 x 852.

What changes

  • Header: four stacked headings become one row. The 60pt thumb shows Daisy in the picked pot, so it is also the preview. Title "Repot Daisy", one subline "Pick a pot. Changing pots is free."
  • New pots: "New pots in your shed!" is a small pill with a close button, shown once, and the new pots carry a tiny "New" tag.
  • Tiles: every tile is 108pt high. The name is one line and shrinks to fit. Line 2 shows the state: the goal ("Finish level 3"), a Plus pill, or "In use". The picked pot has a 3pt ring and a check.
  • Pots to earn: a soft ghost pot on a soft dashed tile, with its goal. No lock, no grey wash on the name.
  • Footer: one line names the pick ("Rose pink pot, fresh loam soil"). A tap on a pot to earn or a Plus pot shows its hint there instead, so it is never off screen. The button is 52pt, one line, and names the pot.
  • Scroll: the grid ends above the footer at default size. Soil chips scroll in under a 28pt fade. The scroll view's bottom inset is the footer's height, so nothing hides under it.
