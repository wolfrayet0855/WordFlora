# Reinforcement principles (operant conditioning, applied ethically)

Audience is mixed kid/adult. Rule: use positive reinforcement and shaping; never punishment, loss, or gambling-style schedules.

1. **Immediate, specific, contingent feedback.** Every care action answers at once with a plant-specific line (PlantVoice) plus sound/haptic. Wins give instant visual/sound/haptic payoff.
2. **Continuous reinforcement for new behaviours, then predictable milestones.** First time doing each tending action always rewards; later rewards are fixed-ratio badge tiers (visible thresholds), never random.
3. **Shaping.** Difficulty and help ladder adapt (AssistTuner, level progression); small steps are rewarded before bigger ones.
4. **Gentle consequences, never lost progress.** Owner ruling (2026-11-03): at every age, a plant with no love for 24 h gets rain and loses a petal, one more per day, and with no petals shows as a seed in a pot. This is a visual state only: stage, growth and the collection are never removed, any love restores petals, a seed wakes with a challenge, and Quiet Garden / Calm mode switch it off. Garden Days never decrease, streaks rest instead of reset, and the welcome-back card replaces return guilt.
5. **No variable-ratio rewards that carry value.** Rose of Jericho is a fixed 8-round meter, not a random drop. Random delight (rare visitors, sways) is cosmetic only: no currency, no purchase link, never adjacent to an ad or paywall.
6. **Stopping cues instead of compulsion loops.** Rest card after every 3rd round and 20-minute break prompt; Quiet Garden removes numbers and costs entirely.
7. **Effort praise over outcome praise.** Copy praises care and curiosity, not winning; rewards under 13 never involve ads.
