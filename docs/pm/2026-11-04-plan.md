## (b) THE RULE

1. Each action has its own daily allowance per plant. No action blocks another.
2. Each meter shows its own action's allowance for today:
   - Water: waterings today ÷ `waterPerDay`.
   - Sun: full only if the plant was sunned today.
   - Soil: fed today, or ready.
3. A meter that is not full means its action works and animates. A full meter means that action is done for today.
4. A blocked button shows a done state (V3); a tap says why and what next.
5. Copy is positive, at a grade-2 reading level, with no banned words (miss, only, last, waiting, lost, dead, empty, left, beat, forget). Add "empty" and "left" to `CopySafety`.

**Meter words**
- Water: "Ready for a drink" / "Good" / "Full".
- Sun: "Ready for sun" / "Bright". Rain does not change this.
- Soil: "Ready for soil" / "Fed today".
- Hearts: 1 for a hello, plus 1 per kind of care today, up to 5.

**Blocked lines** (`{name}` is the nickname if set)
- "{name} has had all the water it needs today. Sun or a hello would be nice."
- "{name} soaked up the sun today. More sun tomorrow!"
- "{name} is well fed today. More soil tomorrow."
- "Your can is dry. Win a word to fill it."
- "Out of soil scoops. Find a new plant to get more."

**Economy.** Per-action limits replace the `dailyAbsorbCap` gate. Each day still has a fixed maximum, so growth stays bounded:
- Add `waterPerDay = 2`. Change `waterGrowth` from 4 to 3 (5 with a soil boost).
- Add `feedPerDay = 1`. Change `soilGrowth` from 12 to 4.
- Sun stays once a day; prune keeps its rest; happy repot grows once a day.
- A full day gives 12 GP, or about 16 with a boost. Raise `levelBaseCost` from 180 to 200 so full bloom takes about 14-17 busy days (about 18 today).

New `PlantCollectible` fields, each with a default: `waterDay`, `waterToday`, `lastFedAt`. `DataReconciler` merges them by max. `GardenBackup` carries them.
