### G5 decision: same release
The full Frost & Lantern pack ships in this release, not 1.1. Reasons: (1) it is a winter pack and a 1.1 in late December lands after the season's front; (2) the App Store product is created once with a review screenshot of its real content, and selling a thin pack now and enlarging it later invites refunds and a second review of the product; (3) the art lane is lock-free and already has a pipeline (`WeatherShelterArt` style, `FlowerView` petal parameters, `PlantThinning.json`), so the critical-path cost is only the mount and its tests (~20 builder-hours, two days); (4) the owner allows the extension. **Fallback (decided now, so nobody re-argues it on Sat 11-14):** if the art lane has not delivered the scene, both visitors with checked facts and both plants' stage art accepted on localhost by Fri 11-13 20:00, G5 is skipped, the pack product is **not** submitted, the framework ships dormant (no product from StoreKit means no Seasons row, no outlines) and the whole pack goes to 1.1. A thin pack is never sold.

### Ordering rules that hold across the plan
- **Stored fields:** `lastMistedAt` (G3, last item) is the only new stored field after G0. G2, G4 and G5 add none (lifetime, pack ownership and glasshouse ownership are read from StoreKit every time; the pack badge and the glasshouse plants' progress live in the existing `badgesJSON` and `PlantCollectible` rows). Any builder who finds they need another field stops and reports; one deploy is the rule.
- **Integration:** every gate ends with the integrator merging the gate branch into `3-feat-content-and-polish` with a real merge, building, running the full unit suite and the UI classes the gate touched. Only G6 runs all seven UI batches.
- **Live ads:** AppLovin stays in test mode (AdMob mediation test suite / AppLovin test device) until the owner flips the AdMob mediation group to serve and confirms the partner lists; that flip happens after submission, not before.

## 4. Risks and go/no-go checkpoints

**Risks, ranked**
1. **The machine.** One `xcodebuild` at a time; the full UI suite is 8 h or more on a loaded box. Mitigation: the lock, the watchdog, targeted classes per gate, the full suite only in G0 and G6, no agent builds outside the lock.
2. **StoreKit reds.** Root cause unknown for weeks. Mitigation: first task of G2, diagnosed not guessed, `XCTSkip` with a reason as the floor; `SKTestSession` lifetime and pack tests ride on the same fix.
3. **AppLovin consent and child rules.** A consent mistake is worse than the revenue. Mitigation: test mode until the owner flips live after submission; under-13 "never starts" test; partner lists republished before the flip; `ageRestrictedTreatment` set before `start()`.
4. **CloudKit single deploy.** Any field added after G3 means a second deploy and a second device run. Mitigation: the ordering rule; builders report "no new stored fields" explicitly; the integrator greps `Models/` diffs at every merge.
5. **Pack content timing.** The art lane carries scene, two visitors and two plants' stage art in a week. Mitigation: the decided fallback (dormant framework, pack to 1.1, no thin pack).
6. **App Review.** Three-option paywall must match the review screenshot; pack and redeem surfaces must stay behind the gate for under 13 and show no price on kid screens; the lifetime "cancel your subscription" note; labels must match both SDKs; the age rating re-answered. Mitigation: the G6 review-notes refresh and the owner's device checklist.
